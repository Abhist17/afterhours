STOCKLANA SUBMISSION FORM — AFTERHOURS
=====================================

PROJECT NAME
------------
Afterhours

ONE-LINER (276/280)
-------------------
The risk desk for tokenized stocks on Solana. Paste any wallet holding xStocks and see what it can lose tomorrow, what it has done since Wall Street closed, and how far it has drifted from the allocation you meant to hold — scored in your browser, recorded on-chain by you.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION (4804/5000)
--------------------------------
THE PROBLEM

An xStock is a Solana token that tracks a share. The token trades every hour of every day; the share trades 9:30–4:00 ET on trading days. Between the close and the next open the token's price is a forecast of the reopen, and the holder carries that gap with nobody on the other side. On a Friday night a wallet of tokenized stocks is a book its underlying market cannot price until Monday.

Every brokerage app shows what you have. None shows what you stand to lose, and none has ever had to think about a share that keeps trading after the bell. Afterhours is the risk desk for that book.

WHAT IT DOES

Paste a Solana address or connect a wallet. The desk reads its balances from mainnet — 35 xStocks (every mint verified on-chain: Token-2022, 8 decimals) plus the SOL, cbBTC and stablecoins beside them — and scores the book in the browser from thirty days of hourly prices. Ten panels:

• Score: annualised volatility plus a concentration penalty, 0–100.
• Value at Risk: 95% one-day loss with Expected Shortfall, parametric and historical; the more conservative one headlines.
• Beta to the S&P 500, for the book and every position, against SPYx.
• Holdings: share of value beside share of risk, sparklines, and the NYSE-closed hours shaded on click.
• Trading without the market: each stock's token move since the last official print, summed — "the market opens to this" — and the share of the book's variance that happened while the NYSE was closed.
• Stocks, crypto, cash: COINx, MSTRx, HOODx, CRCLx are equity on paper and crypto beta in practice; the desk says what share of the book is really riding crypto.
• Thirty days: the book scored at every hour with an estimator that saw only what was known then, and a backtest of the VaR against what happened.
• Correlation across everything held.
• What if: move a quarter, half or all of any position into any other asset and re-score instantly, no server.
• If the market gaps: S&P −5%, crypto −30%, the window's own worst day and worst close-to-open, each position by its beta.
• Target and drift: state the allocation you meant to hold, see the drift and the exact orders, each quoted live on Jupiter with price impact and route.
• Your record on Solana: save the targets as an on-chain Policy and record immutable Snapshots, signed by the wallet that owns the book.

Number keys jump between panels, every view is a link, ? defines every figure. Click "A real xStocks wallet" to run it on a $21M mainnet book found through the largest SPYx token accounts.

WHERE SOLANA IS LOAD-BEARING

Reads: xStocks are Token-2022 mints; the page reads any wallet's token accounts under both token programs, no custody, no API key.

Writes: the Afterhours Anchor program (devnet 3hqhzG55EkCjhUYmmCxHWyNGkXi3XJSTEWimkTzVifri) — five instructions, one event. Policy holds targets in bps summing to 10,000, a risk limit and a drift band; Snapshot holds score, value, VaR, drift, equity share, NYSE-open flag and timestamp. Both are owned and signed by the wallet they describe. Every snapshot under a policy emits SnapshotRecorded { breached }.

That event is a credit primitive: a protocol lending against a stock portfolio can subscribe to "this book left its owner's stated policy" instead of watching a price. scripts/watch-breaches.mjs does exactly that — subscribes, decodes, prints BREACH, can POST to a webhook. Replaying devnet shows the author's own record: two readings in policy, one breach.

ARCHITECTURE: NOTHING TO RUN

A static export: a landing page at / and the desk at /dashboard/, on Render with a GitHub Pages mirror. Balances from mainnet in the browser; hourly history refreshed by a GitHub Action to a data branch and bundled as a fallback; every figure computed in the tab; on-chain writes signed by the viewer. Nothing sleeps, nothing cold-starts.

HONEST BY DESIGN

Sampling interval measured from the data, EWMA decay rescaled to it. Unpriced holdings listed, not dropped, with model coverage printed beside the VaR. Thin names where a stale print can sit for hours are flagged and clipped inside the estimators. The VaR is marked against what actually happened.

NUMBERS
